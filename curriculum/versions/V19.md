# V19 — Jersey visibility and evidence extraction

## Goal

Determine when a jersey number is readable and produce high-quality temporal
crops before attempting number recognition.

## Prerequisites

V16 tracks and V13 jersey-visibility annotations.

## Implementation tasks

1. Define front, back, shoulder, occluded, blurred, and not-visible states.
2. Compare box heuristics, pose-relative crops, and learned region detection.
3. Score crop sharpness, scale, orientation, occlusion, and visibility.
4. Retain the best diverse crops per track without unnecessary footage storage.
5. Reject low-quality evidence explicitly.

## Required artifacts

- Jersey-region cropper and visibility classifier
- Tracklet crop contact sheets
- Crop-quality and visibility report
- Privacy/retention behavior tests

## Metrics

Visibility precision/recall, crop IoU or keypoint-relative coverage, readable
digit height, sharpness distribution, useful-crop yield per track, and false
visible rate.

## Failure injection

Folded jerseys, hair or arms covering digits, one visible digit of two, shoulder
numbers, severe blur, low resolution, and scoreboard text near a player.

## Acceptance criteria

Only sufficiently visible evidence passes to recognition, and tracks without a
usable jersey observation retain an explicit rejection reason.
