# V18 — Team and role evidence

## Goal

Classify each track as home, away, official, sideline/other, or unknown without
forcing ambiguous uniform decisions.

## Prerequisites

V16 tracks and V13 team/role annotations.

## Implementation tasks

1. Build color/appearance baselines before selecting a larger model.
2. Aggregate role probabilities over tracklets.
3. Calibrate probabilities and implement `unknown` thresholds.
4. Evaluate by game, uniform combination, lighting, and shot type.
5. Expose evidence to the identity resolver without converting it into identity.

## Required artifacts

- Tracklet classifier
- Confusion and reliability diagrams
- Per-game calibration report
- Uniform hard-example gallery

## Metrics

Macro-F1, per-class precision/recall, official false-positive rate, unknown
coverage, ECE, Brier score, and probability stability over a track.

## Failure injection

Similar colors, alternate uniforms, shadows, rain, color casts, sideline coats,
official stripes, and partial-body crops.

## Acceptance criteria

Team evidence improves candidate filtering on held-out games without violating
the identity precision gate when uniforms are ambiguous.
