# V21 — Calibrated player identity fusion

## Goal

Combine independent evidence into a canonical player belief while refusing to
label ambiguous tracks.

## Prerequisites

V18 team/role evidence, V20 number distributions, canonical roster mappings,
and V16 temporal tracks.

## Implementation tasks

1. Generate candidates from active rosters and canonical player links.
2. Fuse jersey, team, role, field position, lineup, play context, and temporal
   continuity without treating roster membership as proof.
3. Record candidate scores and evidence provenance.
4. Calibrate identity probabilities on held-out games.
5. Select display thresholds from a precision–coverage curve.
6. Add hysteresis and identity-revocation behavior.

## Required artifacts

- Versioned identity resolver
- Per-track belief timeline
- Reliability and precision–coverage plots
- False-identity review packet and threshold decision record

## Metrics

Displayed identity precision, coverage, false labels per game, top-k accuracy,
ECE, Brier score, NLL, time to identify, revocation time, and accuracy by
evidence combination.

## Failure injection

Duplicate numbers across teams, incomplete rosters, wrong provider maps,
conflicting evidence, substitutions, stale lineup data, and a track handoff.

## Acceptance criteria

The resolver meets predeclared identity precision and false-label gates on
untouched games. It emits `unknown` when confidence or candidate margin is
insufficient.
