# V20 — Temporal jersey-number recognition

## Goal

Estimate a calibrated distribution over jersey numbers by combining evidence
across a track rather than trusting one OCR result.

## Prerequisites

V19 quality-scored jersey crops and V13 number annotations.

## Implementation tasks

1. Establish a generic OCR or digit-classification baseline.
2. Train or adapt a sequence recognizer for one- and two-digit numbers.
3. Aggregate crop logits over time with quality weighting.
4. Preserve alternatives, uncertainty, and `not visible`, not only top-1.
5. Compare single-frame, best-frame, and temporal aggregation.

## Required artifacts

- Number-recognition adapter
- Per-track probability records
- Temporal-versus-single-frame experiment
- Number confusion matrix and failure gallery

## Metrics

Exact-match accuracy, character error rate, visible-number recall, top-k
accuracy, calibration, abstention coverage, time to stable number, and accuracy
by digit count and crop quality.

## Failure injection

Similar digits, partial two-digit numbers, mirrored replay imagery, conflicting
frames, low-quality majority evidence, and a late clean observation.

## Acceptance criteria

Temporal recognition measurably beats the declared single-frame baseline and
does not become more confident when evidence conflicts.
