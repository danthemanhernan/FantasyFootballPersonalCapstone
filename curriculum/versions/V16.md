# V16 — Online multi-object tracking

## Goal

Maintain stable player track IDs through ordinary motion and short occlusions.

## Prerequisites

V15 timestamped detections and V14 reset events.

## Implementation tasks

1. Integrate ByteTrack as the baseline.
2. Compare BoT-SORT or OC-SORT on the same detections.
3. Reset tracker state on scene boundaries.
4. Record births, deaths, associations, confidence, and occlusion recovery.
5. Normalize identity switches per player-minute and game.
6. Produce TrackEval-compatible output and a visual failure reel.

## Required artifacts

- Tracker adapter interface
- Track JSONL and rendered track-ID video
- TrackEval report and tracker decision record

## Metrics

HOTA, DetA, AssA, LocA, IDF1, identity switches, fragmentation, mostly tracked,
mostly lost, reacquisition time, wrong-player handoffs, and tracker latency.

## Failure injection

Crossing players, pile-ups, occlusion, abrupt acceleration, camera pans, missed
detection sequences, and a replay cut.

## Acceptance criteria

The selected tracker improves association evidence without violating the
real-time budget, and no track survives a scene reset.
